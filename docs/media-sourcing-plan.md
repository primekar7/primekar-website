# Media Sourcing Plan

How each missing/unverified asset (`media-inventory.md`) will be obtained with documented rights. **No acquisition or generation happens without owner approval.**

## Priority order

1. **PrimeKar-owned first** — request originals: logo SVG + transparent on-dark; real fleet photography (each vehicle bookable); clean app screenshots; Calgary/YYC photos if owned.
2. **Licensed stock** (only where owned assets don't exist) — Alberta destination photography (Banff/Canmore/Lake Louise/Jasper), generic Calgary/YYC where needed. Record licence source, permitted channels/territory/duration, attribution, modification terms in `media-inventory.md`.
3. **Official third-party artwork** — Apple App Store + Google Play badges per current official guidelines (v7 §25); official product frames or an approved generic device frame (Section 12 device-frame rule).
4. **Generated imagery** — last resort, destination illustrations only, under all v7 §13 conditions (clearly illustrative, no false conditions, no invented PrimeKar vehicle at a real pickup point, no implication PrimeKar ran the trip, owner-approved). **Not** for the actual fleet.

## Rules
Document rights **before** use. No reference-site/competitor/Google-Images assets. Optimize (AVIF/WebP, responsive, width/height) at implementation while preserving owned masters unchanged.

## Open
Everything above is pending owner supply/approval. No media (beyond the two logos) is production-cleared.
